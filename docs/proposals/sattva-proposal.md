## Page 2 — The Land Division · 17 capabilities

*Drop a Google pin. Get everything. In minutes.* — pulled, cross-checked and scored from every government source, then watched around the clock.

1. Pin → survey number — village, hobli, taluk & district
2. Present owner & RTC — holdings, shares & mutation history
3. Disputes & court cases — stays, injunctions & revenue matters
4. Encumbrance & history — every transaction & document number
5. Certified sale deeds — auto-applied for and retrieved
6. Conversion status — agri → non-agri, read from the deed (see `feasibility/F6-conversion.md`)
7. CDP master-plan zoning — superimposed on the map
8. Village-map overlay — survey boundaries, lakes & nalas
9. Proximity risk flags — graveyards, govt land & water bodies
10. Unified parcel profile — every record joined, gaps flagged
11. Fees & payments — government fees via a prepaid wallet
12. Kannada intelligence — documents translated & structured
13. 24/7 monitoring — every tracked parcel, always watched
14. Litigation screening — across all current & past owners
15. Dynamic growth & risk score — weighted to the client's view
16. Favourites & rejection tracking — full history & auto re-checks
17. Complete due-diligence report — the path to go / no-go
- *(+ Cross-verified & discrepancies highlighted — sources reconciled, never hidden)*

One join key — the **survey number** — unifies Dishaank, Bhoomi, Kaveri, the CDP master plan and e-Chavadi. Coverage: **Bangalore Urban, Bangalore Rural & Mysore**, monitored 24/7.
